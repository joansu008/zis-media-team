# Content rules

- The opening must establish relevance quickly.
- The structure needs one central claim and a readable progression.
- The title must match the actual content and selected topic.
- Unsupported absolutes, invented numbers, and invented quotations fail review.
- A short-video script should be speakable, concise, and end with a useful conclusion or next action.
- Long-video candidates must retain source time ranges; unknown ranges must be explicit.
